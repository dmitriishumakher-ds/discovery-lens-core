INTERNAL FEEDBACK NOTE — AM Team Meeting
Submitted by: Sophie Chen, AM
Date: 18 Mar 2025

Summarising themes from Q4 2024 and Q1 2025 account interactions for product team review:

1. PERMISSION/SHARING — most common reason for escalations and renewal anxiety. The guest/member sharing flow creates confusion across all experience levels. Two-click access-scope preview at point of sharing would resolve most reported incidents. Request: surface this to design team as P1 item.

2. LARGE DATABASE PERFORMANCE — affecting mid-market accounts with mature workspaces. Typically surfaces around 1,500–3,000+ records. Accounts in this range are highest churn risk. Vanta is the clearest example but not the only one. Workarounds offered (pre-built views, database splitting) are developer-tier recommendations that operations and non-technical users can't execute alone.

3. ONBOARDING TIME — consistently 2–4 weeks for a new user to reach basic proficiency. This is structural overhead for teams with turnover. Worse for companies in high-churn sectors (consulting, agencies). Two accounts mentioned the irony of "having a Notion guide for how to use Notion." Interactive in-product onboarding for new users (not just a welcome page) would reduce this materially.

4. MOBILE — creative and field-work sectors are being lost or slowed on this point. AE team consistently has to caveat mobile in demos. Mobile is not a nice-to-have for a significant portion of our addressable market. Android specifically worse than iOS and causing support volume.

5. AI UPSELL FRICTION — minor but consistent. Users on plans without AI features encounter AI prompts in-product and either raise a ticket or contact their AM confused. Admin toggle to hide AI features for non-AI plans would reduce noise.
